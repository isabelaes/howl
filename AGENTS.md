# Howl project notes

- Howl is a social app for NC State students to share short posts about campus life.
- Keep the working app in the project root; keep design experiments in numbered rounds inside `explorations/`.
- The owner is a beginner and does not use the terminal. Give simple, one-step-at-a-time guidance and avoid asking them to run terminal commands.
- Keep the app plain and unstyled unless the owner asks for design work.
